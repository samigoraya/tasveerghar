Questions people ask

Where is Tasveer Ghar in Sialkot?

Tasveer Ghar is at 113 Aziz Bhati Shaheed Road, Sadar Bazar, Sialkot Cantt 51310, Pakistan.

What can I rent at Tasveer Ghar?

Tasveer Ghar is a photo rental studio with 20 indoor and outdoor sets that you can rent for your shoot.

What kinds of shoots are the sets suited for?

The sets are made for weddings, bridal and mehndi shoots, engagements, fashion and modelling, product shoots, portraits and family photos.

What are the opening hours of Tasveer Ghar?

Tasveer Ghar is open 7 days a week, from 10 am to 1 am.

How do I book a set at Tasveer Ghar?

Send us your date on WhatsApp, confirm the booking with us, then come to the studio and shoot.
